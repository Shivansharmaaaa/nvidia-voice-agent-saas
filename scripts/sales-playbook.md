# Sales Playbook

## Live Demo Script (15 minutes)

### Opening (2 min)
"Thanks for your interest in our AI voice agent platform. Today I'll show you how we help companies automate customer calls while maintaining quality and reducing costs by 60-80%."

**Key Points:**
- We use NVIDIA's latest open-source models (Nemotron, Magpie)
- Real-time voice processing with <500ms latency
- Multi-tenant SaaS with enterprise-grade security

### Demo Flow (10 min)

**1. Agent Creation (3 min)**
- Show agent creation wizard
- Configure business persona, knowledge base, voice settings
- Emphasize: "No coding required, 5-minute setup"

**2. Live Call Demo (4 min)**
- Make test call to demo agent
- Show real-time transcription
- Demonstrate natural conversation flow
- Highlight: "This is live, not pre-recorded"

**3. Analytics Dashboard (3 min)**
- Show call logs and transcripts
- Display ROI calculator: "You're saving $X vs human agents"
- Show conversion metrics and health scores

### Closing (3 min)
- Address common objections (see below)
- Discuss pricing tiers
- Next steps: "Would you like to start with a 14-day free trial?"

## Competitive Positioning

### vs Human Call Centers

**Our Advantages:**
- 60-80% cost reduction
- 24/7 availability, no breaks
- Consistent quality, no bad days
- Instant scaling
- Full call analytics and insights

**When to Use Humans:**
- Complex emotional situations
- High-value sales (enterprise deals)
- Regulatory requirements for human oversight

**Positioning:** "We handle 80% of routine calls, freeing your team for high-value interactions."

### vs OpenAI Voice Agents

**Our Advantages:**
- Open-source stack (no vendor lock-in)
- Self-hosted option for data sovereignty
- Lower costs (no per-token pricing)
- Customizable models per use case
- Multi-tenant architecture built-in

**Their Advantages:**
- GPT-4 quality (we use Nemotron 3 Nano)
- Larger model options

**Positioning:** "We offer enterprise control, predictable costs, and open-source flexibility."

### vs Other AI Voice Startups

**Our Advantages:**
- NVIDIA-backed open-source models
- Production-ready infrastructure
- Enterprise features (SSO, audit logs, dedicated GPU)
- Transparent pricing with healthy margins
- Full-stack solution (not just API)

**Positioning:** "We're the enterprise-ready platform built on open-source, not a closed API."

## Objection Handling

### "We need human agents for quality"

**Response:**
"I understand. Our platform actually improves quality through consistency. Every call follows your best practices, and we provide human escalation for complex cases. Most customers see 90%+ resolution rates without human intervention. Would you like to see our quality metrics?"

### "What about data security?"

**Response:**
"Security is our top priority. We're SOC 2 Type I compliant, offer SSO, IP allowlisting, and can deploy on-premise for sensitive data. All calls are encrypted, and we provide full audit logs. Many enterprise customers choose us specifically for our security posture."

### "We already have a call center"

**Response:**
"Perfect! We're designed to augment, not replace. Your team handles complex cases while we handle routine inquiries 24/7. This actually improves your team's job satisfaction by removing repetitive calls. Most customers see 60-80% cost savings while improving customer satisfaction."

### "The pricing seems high"

**Response:**
"Let me show you the ROI calculator. At $0.15/minute overage, you're paying $9/hour for an AI agent vs $25-35/hour for a human. Plus no benefits, training, or turnover costs. Most customers see payback in the first month. What's your current cost per call?"

### "We need custom integrations"

**Response:**
"Absolutely. We offer API access on Growth plan and custom integrations on Enterprise. We've integrated with Salesforce, Zendesk, HubSpot, and many others. What systems do you need to connect?"

## Sales Talking Points

### Value Propositions

1. **Cost Reduction:** "Reduce call center costs by 60-80%"
2. **Scalability:** "Handle 10x more calls without hiring"
3. **Quality:** "Consistent, professional service 24/7"
4. **Insights:** "Full analytics on every customer interaction"
5. **Speed:** "Deploy in days, not months"

### Use Cases

- **Customer Support:** Handle 80% of routine inquiries
- **Appointment Booking:** Automate scheduling 24/7
- **Lead Qualification:** Qualify and route leads instantly
- **Order Status:** Real-time order updates
- **FAQ Handling:** Answer common questions instantly

### ROI Calculator Inputs

- Current calls per month
- Average call duration
- Cost per human agent hour
- Current resolution rate
- Target resolution rate

**Output:**
- Monthly savings
- Annual savings
- Payback period
- Cost per call comparison

## Qualification Questions

1. How many calls do you handle per month?
2. What's your current cost per call?
3. What percentage of calls are routine vs complex?
4. Do you have 24/7 coverage needs?
5. What's your current customer satisfaction score?
6. Are you looking to scale up or reduce costs?
7. What integrations do you need?
8. What's your timeline for implementation?

## Closing Techniques

### Assumptive Close
"Great! Let's get you set up with a 14-day free trial. I'll send you the onboarding link."

### Urgency Close
"We're offering early adopter pricing through [date]. After that, prices increase 20%."

### Value Close
"Based on your volume, you'll save $X per month. That's $Y annually. When would you like to start?"

### Trial Close
"Let's start with a 14-day trial. No credit card required. You can test with real calls and see the ROI yourself."
